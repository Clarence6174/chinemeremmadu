About

Background

Freelance Machine Learning Engineer with hands-on experience designing and deploying end-to-end ML systems — from raw data ingestion to production inference. I work across the full stack: data pipelines, model training, evaluation, and cloud deployment.

Specialization

Deep learning, MLOps, and distributed backend infrastructure. I focus on building systems that are reproducible, monitored, and designed to stay reliable under real production load — not just in a Jupyter notebook.

Approach

Every architectural decision is justified by the problem constraints — dataset size, latency budget, compute cost, and interpretability requirements. I choose the simplest model that solves the problem verifiably well.

Open Source

I build in public. Several of my tools exist because I needed them and they didn't. Open source is how I verify my work against real-world constraints beyond my own projects.
